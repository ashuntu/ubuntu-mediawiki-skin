This page stresses the skin's content layout: wide tables should scroll horizontally within the content area instead of breaking the grid, and long unbreakable strings should not push the sidebar or TOC off-screen. Resize the browser below each breakpoint (~1120px, ~768px) and check that the header, TOC, and footer stay intact.
